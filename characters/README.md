# New characters (proposals)

Two new characters you supplied, ready to join Milo & Pip. The names, roles and personalities are suggestions; change them freely.
To animate either one in full (walking, reacting), first generate a character sheet from the prompt below, the same way as Milo and Pip.

## Lemon: Pip's little cousin (`bunny_yellow_hood.jpg`)
- **Look:** a tiny round cream-white bunny with short white ears poking out of a fluffy butter-yellow hood, big round yellow glasses, dark shiny eyes, rosy cheeks and a short yellow cape-coat with one round button. She is smaller than Pip, about Milo's height, so the two bunnies are easy to tell apart (Pip is lavender with a green scarf; Lemon is white in yellow).
- **Personality:** careful, curious and a little shy. She notices tiny things through her big glasses and often spots the clue the others miss. She gets nervous when things are loud or new.
- **Voice:** small, soft and slightly breathy; speaks a little slower than Pip.
- **Good first episode:** idea #6, "Milo and the New Friend" (jealousy / including others). Lemon arrives for a visit, Pip spends the day with her, and Milo feels left out. Lemon also fits #3 (fear), sharing it with Pip.

**Reuse line (paste into every prompt she appears in):**
```
Lemon, a tiny round cream-white bunny with short white ears poking out of a fluffy butter-yellow hood, big round yellow glasses, dark shiny eyes, rosy cheeks and a short yellow cape-coat with one round button
```
**Character sheet prompt:**
```
Character reference sheet of Lemon, a tiny round cream-white bunny for a preschool cartoon: very round body, short legs, short white ears poking out of a fluffy butter-yellow knitted hood, big round yellow glasses, dark shiny eyes with highlights, rosy cheeks, a little pink nose, and a short yellow cape-coat with one round button. Front, side and back views plus four expressions: shy smile, curious peering through her glasses, nervous, happy giggle. Soft rounded 3D preschool cartoon style matching a yellow duckling and a lavender bunny, gentle soft lighting, plain light-gray background, no text.
```

## Kai: the show's host (`boy_orange_shirt.jpg`)
- **Look:** a friendly little boy with spiky black hair, big brown eyes, rosy cheeks, a wide smile and an orange polo shirt.
- **Role (suggested):** opens each episode in a park setting ("Hi friends! Today Milo and Pip have a windy day..."), asks the viewer question, and closes with the lesson. He is live-action-style 3D, so as a host he frames the cartoon world rather than living inside it.
- **Voice:** bright, warm, about 7 years old.
- **With the single image we have:** a chest-up talking shot works for intros and outros. A sheet gives more poses.

**Character sheet prompt:**
```
Character reference sheet of Kai, a friendly little boy about 6 years old for a preschool cartoon: spiky black hair, big brown eyes with highlights, thick dark eyebrows, rosy cheeks, a wide happy smile, an orange polo shirt, blue shorts and white sneakers. Front, side and back views plus four expressions: big smile, curious, thinking with hand on chin, cheering. Soft rounded 3D cartoon style, gentle soft lighting, plain light-gray background, no text.
```
