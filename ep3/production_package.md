**Story.** Mr. Bumble has baked twelve star cookies, one for each neighbor at the garden party. Milo sneaks one, and when Mr. Bumble counts eleven and asks "Did somebody take a cookie?", Milo says "Not me!" The yellow icing on Pip's nose makes her look guilty instead, and Mr. Bumble's flour is all gone, so someone will be left out. Milo's tummy feels heavy, and the Narrator names it: guilt. Mr. Bumble sits beside him and tells how he once broke his mom's teapot and hid it: "The truth feels lighter." Milo tells the truth, apologizes to Pip for letting her be blamed, and feels lighter himself. Pip fetches flour, they bake one lopsided star together, and the tray reaches twelve again.

**Why this works for preschoolers.** The problem is something a child can count: 12, then 11, then 12 again. Guilt is shown as a body feeling (a sinking posture and a heavy tummy), so children can recognize it in themselves. The grown-up models honesty by sharing his own mistake, not by lecturing. The fix is a shared job that ends in a joke.

**Characters.**
- **Milo** (yellow duckling, blue boots): bouncy and quick. When he feels guilty, his body sinks, his tuft droops and he hides his wings. When he tells the truth, his body rises back up.
- **Pip** (lavender bunny, sage scarf): kind and calm. Her ears droop when she's blamed and pop up when Milo says sorry.
- **Mr. Bumble** (big round yellow neighbor, blue suspenders): warm, slow, a bit clumsy, deep voice. He kneels or sits so he's closer to the little ones' eye level.
- Voices: Milo is bright and higher, Pip warm and soft, Mr. Bumble deep and cheerful, and the Narrator a slow storyteller. All speak at about 2 words per second.

**Key prop: the cookie tray.** It holds twelve yellow-iced star cookies in three rows of four. States: twelve → eleven with an empty spot and crumbs → twelve again with one lopsided star.

**Sound plan.**
- **Counting ticks:** a soft tick on each count in S11–S12 and again in S47, the payoff.
- **"Lighter" motif:** three rising chimes under S28 and S39.
- **Clock tick:** plays during the quiet moments (S20–S21) with no music.
- **Flour POOF:** at S08 and again at S52.
- **Theme:** the same Milo & Pip theme as Episodes 1–2.

**Captions (edit only).** Rounded cream pills with the key word in orange, max 4 words, 2–3 s, kept away from faces: UH-OH!, GUILT, TELLING THE TRUTH and THE TRUTH FEELS LIGHTER.

The remaining ideas from Episode 2's list still apply: thunder at a sleepover (fear), one red bucket (sharing), the seed that won't grow (patience), the new friend (jealousy, a natural first episode for Lemon), and the rainy picnic (disappointment, where Mr. Bumble lends his big umbrella).
