One prompt per shot, keyed to the timeline. Each prompt names who is visible, what they do, the setting, framing, camera and duration.

**Shot types:**
- **generate clip:** a new 5–8 s clip.
- **HOLD:** generate the clip, then hold or extend the reaction in the edit.
- **STILL:** generate one image and do a slow pan or push in the editor.

**Production settings:**
- 16:9 at 1080p, with **clip audio off**. Voices, music, sound effects and captions go on separate tracks.
- **Reuse the background:** every shot is in Mr. Bumble's kitchen. Generate the kitchen plate once (below) and use it as a start or reference image.
- Attach the character sheets for Milo, Pip and Mr. Bumble in every shot they appear in.
- The cookie tray has three states: **twelve** (S01), **eleven with one empty spot** (S02–S46) and **full again with one lopsided star** (S47 on). Keep it consistent.
