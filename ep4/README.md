# Episode 4: Counting 1 to 10 with Milo & Pip

A learning video built for search: counting 1–10 with objects, number words and "Your turn!" repeat pauses, then a count-up recap and a 10-to-1 rocket countdown.

- `ep4_counting_1_to_10.mp4`: main video (2:52, 1920×1080, about -14 LUFS)
- `shorts/`: three vertical Shorts (1080×1920) cut from the same timeline
- `thumbnail_ep4.png`: custom thumbnail (1280×720)
- `ep4_captions.srt`: captions
- `UPLOAD_AND_GROWTH.md`: copy-paste title, description, chapters and tags, Shorts posting plan, and an honest diagnosis of why Episodes 1–3 got few views
- `build/make.py`: one script rebuilds everything (`timeline`, `audio`, `video`, `shorts`, `thumb`). Change `THINGS` or the colors to make Counting 11–20, Shapes and so on.
