1. Episode plan
Title	Milo and the Missing Rainbow Color
Runtime	6:00 exactly (360 s), 16:9, 1920 × 1080
Target age	2 to 5 years (preschool)
Visual style	Soft rounded 3D cartoon, bright pastel colors, soft daylight, simple uncluttered backgrounds, one clear action per shot, slow gentle camera.
Learning goal	Naming colors: red, green, blue (plus yellow, orange and purple along the way). Teamwork.
Problem shown at	0:06 ("A stripe is missing!") and 0:11 (close-up of the gap), inside the first 15 seconds.
Summary. After a rain shower, Milo the duck and Pip the bunny spot a rainbow, but the blue stripe is missing. A glowing picture card in the grass starts a trail of clues: red tulips, a green lily pond, then tall yellow sunflowers. Along the way they ask viewers to name the colors they see. The last card points to a blue pinwheel at the end of the fence. Milo can't spin it alone, but when the friends blow together, a ribbon of blue light flies up and fills the rainbow. They recap the colors and wave goodbye.

Rainbow note. For simplicity the rainbow has six stripes: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple. Until the end, the blue stripe is an empty gap between green and purple.
