## Series rules (for every future brief)
- One main feeling per episode, named once by the Narrator in simple words.
- A clear, concrete problem the child can see, such as a torn blade, one bucket or a missing cookie.
- The ending pays off something seen or heard at the start, the way the wind sound pays off here.
- Ask one viewer question with a 3–4 s pause.
- A short recurring line per episode, used twice.
- Milo and Pip keep their designs, voices and personalities. Pip is often the calm one, but let her own a feeling too (as in Episode 3).
