One prompt per shot, keyed to the timeline. Each prompt names who is visible, what they do, the setting, framing, camera and duration.

**Shot types:**
- **generate clip:** a new 5–8 s clip.
- **HOLD:** generate the clip, then hold or extend the reaction in the edit (freeze the last frame or slow it to 80%).
- **STILL:** generate one image and do a slow pan or push in the editor.
- **REUSE:** no new generation.

**Production settings:**
- Set every clip to 16:9 at 1080p, with **clip audio off**. Voices, music, sound effects and captions all go on separate tracks in the editor.
- **Reuse the background:** all porch shots (S01–S04 and S12–S54) share one porch plate, and S05–S11 share one craft-corner plate. Generate each plate once as a still and use it as a start or reference image so the set doesn't drift.
- Attach the 2D character sheets as references in every shot where Milo or Pip appears.
- Shots under 5 s: generate 5 s and trim, as noted.
