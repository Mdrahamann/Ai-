**Story.** Milo and Pip build a paper windmill. As Milo presses on the last blade, a gust rips it, and Milo blurts "You let go!" Pip quietly shows she is still holding her end. A rewind to that morning shows how proud Milo was, plus a funny moment when a blade glues itself to his wing. Back on the porch, Milo rushes to fix it with tape and makes the tear bigger, and the scene goes quiet. Pip says "Pause. Breathe. Tell me." and then gives him space. Milo notices her droopy ears and remembers the words himself. He breathes, tells her what upset him, and makes a specific apology. They patch the blade with a scrap of rainbow paper, and the same wind that tore it now makes it spin.
